# Meeting prep — the battle plan

## Hard rules

- Facts about the company and attendees come from enrichment, their site, the CRM and past
  calls. Never from memory. An unconfirmed fact is cut, not hedged.
- Read the whole deal history first: CRM description log, prior debriefs, `clients/<slug>/`.
- Check the calendar and inbox for the last 14 days. The CRM lags.

## Read first

`company-context`, `product-context`, `sales-playbook`, `sales-call-scorer/PLAYBOOK.md`.

## Gather (in parallel; degrade gracefully when a source is missing)

1. CRM: deal, stage, contacts with roles, description log, open tasks.
2. Enrichment: company facts, each attendee's role and tenure.
3. Their site: what they sell, recent news.
4. Prior calls: `clients/<slug>/calls/` and transcripts.

## Output

```
MEETING: [company] — [date, time] — [stage]
WIN CONDITION: [the one outcome that makes this a win]

SETUP: two sentences on where the deal is and why this meeting matters.

ATTENDEES
- [name], [title]: likely push → how to win or deflect

LANDMINES
- [risk] → [counter]

PLAYS TO RUN (from PLAYBOOK.md, by number)

PIN DOWN: [the qualification gaps this call must close]
DO NOT: [what would lose the room]

PREP TO-DO: [owner, item]
```

## Quality bar

A rep should be able to read it in two minutes in the hallway and walk in knowing the one thing
they must leave with.
