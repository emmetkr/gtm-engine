# Sales playbook

## Read first

- `references/commercial.md` — what we sell and what it costs. Quote from it and nowhere else.
- `company-context` — who buys and why.
- `product-context` — what exists. Never claim beyond it.

## The calls

<!-- One block per call in your motion. Keep the stage names the same as crm-records. -->

### Call 1 — Discovery

- **Purpose:** qualify, find the acute pain, earn the next meeting.
- **Win condition:** a dated next meeting with the person who feels the pain.
- **Must learn:** budget reality, who decides, the trigger event, how they buy.

### Call 2 — Demo

- **Purpose:** show the product against the pain they named. Demo to the pain, not the menu.
- **Win condition:** a trial or a proposal meeting on the calendar.

### Call 3 — Proposal

- **Purpose:** walk the options; let them choose.
- **Win condition:** a chosen option and a named path to signature.

## Objections

`references/objections.md` — each with the scripted answer.

## Pricing

Never improvise a number. If a buyer asks for something `commercial.md` does not cover, say you
will come back with it, and file it in `NEEDS.md`. A custom deal is recorded in
`clients/<slug>/custom-offer.md` with a variance table; it never edits `commercial.md`.
