# Humanize copy

## Kill list

- Em dashes in outgoing copy. Use a comma, a period or parentheses.
- "Not X, but Y" constructions and "it's not just X" openers. Say what it is.
- "Happy to", "I hope this finds you well", "just circling back", "leverage", "unlock",
  "seamless", "robust", "game-changer", "in today's fast-paced world".
- A punchy one-line tagline at the end of a paragraph.
- Triplets by reflex ("fast, simple, and powerful").
- Hedging stacks ("may potentially help to").

## Rhythm

- Vary sentence length. One short sentence after two long ones.
- One idea per sentence.
- Use the buyer's own words where you have them (`clients/<slug>/intelligence.md`).

## Rule

Do not invent claims while rewriting. Restructure, cut, and reorder the words you were given.
