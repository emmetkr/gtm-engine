# Who reads this, and are we asking them for something?

Answer both before writing. The ask decides the register; the audience decides the claims.

| Tier | Reader | Ask | Sounds like | Explains |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Persuasive** | prospect, client, investor | yes: book, buy, sign | short, specific, their words back to them | only what moves the decision |
| **Informative** | client, partner | no: they need to understand | plain, complete, neutral | enough to act without a call |
| **Internal** | team, agents | sometimes | dense, exact, cited | whatever prevents the next mistake |

## Binding on every tier

- One page where possible. Every figure sourced, or marked as a proposal.
- Nothing invented: client-facing words come from the founder, past decks or transcripts.
- Plain status before narrative in internal updates.
